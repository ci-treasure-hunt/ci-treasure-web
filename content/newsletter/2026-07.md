Hi there,

If you've been getting our Telegram announcements but haven't seen the website yet, please visit **[citreasurehunt.com](/)**. It's a growing, global directory built for contact improvisation specifically: a searchable calendar of CI events, a map of communities, and, brand new, a directory of over 50 venues around the world that regularly host CI, with real photos and addresses, not just names.

Three places to start:\
🗓️ [citreasurehunt.com](/): the full events calendar\
🌍 [citreasurehunt.com/communities](/communities): find your local scene\
🏠 [citreasurehunt.com/venues](/venues): spaces that host CI regularly, worldwide

## A few things happening this month

🇲🇽 **Cuerpo Relacional: Encuentro de improvisación de contacto** (Mexico City, Jul 27–Aug 2)\
A week at UNAM's Centro Cultural Universitario: morning classes open to beginners, afternoon labs for experienced practitioners, 56 contact hours in total. [citreasurehunt.com/events/owXR-cuerpo-relacional-encuentro-de-improvisacion-de-contacto](/events/owXR-cuerpo-relacional-encuentro-de-improvisacion-de-contacto)

🇬🇧 **The Dragon Orchard Jam** (Putley, UK, Jul 24–28)\
A daily intensive with Charlie Morrissey, set in a working organic orchard in Herefordshire. Charlie brings 35+ years of practice, with a lineage running through Steve Paxton, Lisa Nelson, and Siobhan Davies. [citreasurehunt.com/events/A0bj-the-dragon-orchard-jam](/events/A0bj-the-dragon-orchard-jam)

🇮🇩 **Physical Forces and How to Fall in Love with Them** (Ubud, Indonesia, Jul 25–Aug 2)\
A structured deep-dive into CI's physical principles, building from fundamentals to flying and shoulder work, using a visual symbol system to map the practice. [citreasurehunt.com/events/pFrc-physical-forces-and-how-to-fall-in-love-with-them](/events/pFrc-physical-forces-and-how-to-fall-in-love-with-them)

## How to support the network

📍 **Missing an event, venue, or community?** Just reply to this email, or post it in our Telegram or Facebook group and we'll add it.

🔑 **Are you a teacher or organizer?** We're testing sign-in: claim your profile, add a bio and photo, manage your own events (weekend workshops included), ahead of a full public launch. You're one of the first people seeing it, so it might be rough around the edges: [citreasurehunt.com/auth](/auth). Poke around, break things, tell us what's confusing.

I am truly excited for all the good stuff coming next for CI Treasure Hunt, hopefully making the CI universe easier to explore.

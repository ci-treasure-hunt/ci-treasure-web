Hi there,

It's been three months since my last email. Summer turned into autumn, and CI Treasure Hunt kept growing in the background. Here's what changed.

If you're new here (almost half of you joined since July): CI Treasure Hunt is a worldwide directory for Contact Improvisation. Events, local communities, venues and teachers, all in one place at [citreasurehunt.com](/).

## From festivals to your weekly jam

When this started in June, it was a list of festivals and intensives. In July, weekend workshops joined. Now one-day workshops can be listed too, and organizers can add their regular weekly jams and classes, with all the dates in one listing. [Sign in](/auth?next=/dashboard) with Google or an email link, claim or create your profile, and add your own events.

## Communities have moved home

The communities on the website used to come from an Airtable list behind the scenes, and new ones were added through Airtable forms. In September all of it moved into the website's own database. Adding or updating a community now happens right on the website, and the people who run a community can keep it up to date themselves. The old Airtable list and forms are no longer updated, so if you bookmarked them, use [citreasurehunt.com/communities](/communities) instead. 321 communities in 68 countries so far.

## More to explore

👤 **Teachers:** over 850 teachers and organizers at [citreasurehunt.com/teachers](/teachers), each linked to their events.

🏠 **Venues:** the directory has doubled, to over 100 studios and spaces that host CI, at [citreasurehunt.com/venues](/venues).

📖 **Guides:** what CI is, jam etiquette, preparing for a festival, finding CI where you live, and more at [citreasurehunt.com/guides](/guides).

📱 **Put it on your phone:** open [citreasurehunt.com](/) on your phone and tap "Add to Home Screen" at the top of the page. It opens like an app, no app store needed.

## New ways to follow along

Post a multi-day event in the Americas or Asia-Pacific group and I'll add it to the website for you (Americas until November 30, Asia-Pacific until December 31).

🌊 **Our first group beyond CI:** [Water Dance & Aquatic Movement Events Worldwide](https://www.facebook.com/groups/citreasurehuntaqua).

🤖 **Ask your AI assistant:** connect Claude, ChatGPT, Grok or Gemini to CI Treasure Hunt, and it can answer "where can I dance CI in Lisbon next month?" from the live calendar instead of guessing from the web. [How to connect it](/agents)

Hi there,

Between now and New Year's Eve, more than 100 Contact Improvisation festivals, workshops, retreats and long jams start in over 30 countries. Here is one from each continent, and the New Year's gatherings.

## Six continents, six picks

🇪🇬 **[Contact Improvisation with Giulio Piazzi](/events/XEvu)** (Cairo, Egypt, Nov 5–8)\
Four days at ÉLA in downtown Cairo, from floorwork and weight sharing to falling, lifts and an open jam on the last day. Open to anyone curious, with or without CI experience.

🇦🇺 **[ReWoven](/events/RwV6)** (Glenrock, near Newcastle, Australia, Nov 5–8)\
A four-day residential retreat with Emily Bowman, moving between studio, sea and bush. Not for learning CI from scratch but for deepening a practice you already have; pay-what-you-can places available.

🇨🇦 **[Jam Annuel de Montréal](/events/Mtl6)** (Montreal, Nov 6–8)\
The 41st edition, and the first at Espace Marie Chouinard. Kirstie Simson performs and teaches, there are jams with live musicians, and Andrew Harwood shows CI films.

🇬🇧 **[Winter Warmer Festival](/events/WW26)** (Somerset, UK, Nov 26–30)\
The third edition, at a historic country house on the edge of Exmoor. Mornings are four parallel intensives where you stay with one teacher and one small group, from a beginner-friendly class to advanced study of timing.

🇵🇪 **[Encuentro en el Valle Sagrado](/events/q692)** (Cusco, Peru, Dec 12–19)\
The 8th edition, in the Sacred Valley of the Incas. Classes, labs, silent jams and days of dancing in nature, open to people with or without CI experience.

🇮🇩 **[Physical Forces and How to Fall in Love with Them](/events/PhF2)** (Ubud, Bali, Dec 12–20)\
Seven days on the physics behind CI, one principle at a time, from the simplest fundamentals to flying. For dancers with some CI experience.

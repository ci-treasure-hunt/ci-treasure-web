*123 CI festivals & intensives in 34 countries · 243 CI communities in 66 countries*

Hi there,

You're one of the first people to receive this, so thank you for being here early.

I'm Jan, a dancer based in Berlin. I've been building CI Treasure Hunt over the past months: a community-sourced effort to map the global Contact Improvisation world and make it easier to find where CI is happening, wherever you are.

Over the years, I kept running into the same problem. I'd travel somewhere, spend days or weeks there, and only after returning home discover that there had been a local Contact Improvisation community all along. A regular jam, a WhatsApp group, a Telegram chat, a mailing list. But somehow I never found out in time.

The same happened with festivals. There are amazing CI gatherings happening all over the world, but finding them in time often means searching through dozens of (sometimes unorganized) Facebook groups, websites, Telegram channels, and personal recommendations. Planning travels around CI can feel like a treasure hunt.

I started CI Treasure Hunt to make that easier.

The goal is simple: create a community-maintained map of the global CI ecosystem, so people can more easily discover communities, festivals, and opportunities to connect, whether they're traveling across the world or looking for a jam in the next city.

CITH has two pillars:

**CI Communities:** a growing directory of CI resources like websites, chat groups, calendars etc. around the world. [Browse it](/communities) or join the conversation in the [Telegram group](https://t.me/citreasurehunt/13). And, most importantly, please [add any CI community](/communities/new) that is not on our list yet.

**CI Festivals & Intensives list:** a continuously-updated list of multi-day events worldwide. It lives in the [Telegram group](https://t.me/citreasurehunt/174), events are also sourced from our [Facebook group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/citreasurehunt). Snapshots are distributed about monthly in Facebook groups, on Reddit, and from now on, highlights also here in your inbox.

## The June List: 123 events, 34 countries

Three events from Europe, Asia and the Americas caught my eye this month:

🇫🇮 **Midnight Sun CI Festival**, June 10–14, Helsinki.\
Dancing in the northern summer light. Enough said.

🇲🇾 **Contact Festival Kuala Lumpur**, June 15–21.\
Southeast Asia has a lively and established CI scene, and this festival, which is now running its 13th edition, is one of its anchors.

🇨🇴 **Encuentro de CI en Colombia**, June 26–July 5, Villa de Leyva.\
Nine days of workshops, jams, and nature labs in the Colombian highlands, now in its 5th edition, with teachers from Chile, Colombia, and the US.

The full list is in the [Telegram group](https://t.me/citreasurehunt/174).

Know someone who'd find this useful? Forward this email or share the [subscribe link](/newsletter). Every person who joins makes the network a little stronger.

Thank you for being part of our network.

See you on the dance floor,\
